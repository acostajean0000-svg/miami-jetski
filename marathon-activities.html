Marathon — the middle of the Florida Keys

Marathon sits at mile-marker 50 on the Overseas Highway, exactly halfway between Key Largo and Key West. Anglers and divers come for its proximity to Sombrero Reef (one of the healthiest sections of America's only living coral reef) and for sandbar charters near Bahia Honda State Park. The town is also the home of the world-famous Dolphin Research Center and the Turtle Hospital, two non-profit attractions families can pair with a half-day on the water.

Marathon's vibe is quieter than Key West and more wildlife-driven. Most operators launch from the Faro Blanco Marina, the Marathon Yacht Club docks, or 7-Mile Bridge Resort. Snorkel trips run 4-6 hours, sandbar charters 3-6 hours, and private fishing charters are typically all-day (sunrise to mid-afternoon). Bring reef-safe sunscreen — Florida law prohibits oxybenzone and octinoxate within reef-protected waters.

Best time to visit: November through April for calm seas and clear water. June-October brings warmer water (great for snorkeling) but more afternoon storms. Book the iconic Dolphin Encounter swim programs 3-4 weeks ahead — they sell out faster than any other Keys activity.
