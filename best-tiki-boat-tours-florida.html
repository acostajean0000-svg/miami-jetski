Frequently Asked Questions

What is a tiki boat tour?

A tiki boat is a floating tiki bar — a pontoon boat decorated as a tiki bar with a thatched roof, bar-top seating, and a Bluetooth sound system. Most are BYOB (bring your own food and drinks). You cruise calm coastal waterways while relaxing on the floating bar.

Are tiki boat tours BYOB?

Yes — most Florida tiki boats are BYOB (Bring Your Own Beverage). Bring your own cooler, drinks, and snacks. Some operators provide ice or a built-in cooler. Check each operator's policy before booking.

How many people fit on a tiki boat?

Most Florida tiki boats fit 6–10 passengers plus the captain. Some larger pontoon tiki boats hold up to 16 passengers. Private charters are the most common format — you get the whole boat for your group.

Are tiki boats good for bachelorette parties?

Absolutely — tiki boats are one of the most popular bachelorette party activities in Florida. The BYOB floating bar format, coastal views, and group-exclusive private boat make it a perfect party experience.
