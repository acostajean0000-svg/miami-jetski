Exploring Indian Rocks Beach by Golf Cart

Indian Rocks Beach is a quiet, residential Gulf Coast community just south of Clearwater. The barrier island is less crowded than Clearwater Beach or St. Pete Beach, making it a favorite for families. A golf cart lets you roll along Gulf Blvd, stop at any beach access point, and discover local restaurants and shops tucked along the waterway.

Nearby highlights: Indian Rocks Beach Public Access Parks, the waterfront dining along the Intracoastal, and the pristine white-sand Gulf beach that runs the full length of the island.
