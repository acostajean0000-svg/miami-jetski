About Beachside Motorsports – Panama City

Beachside Motorsports brings golf cart rentals to the Panama City area on Florida's Panhandle. Panama City sits at the head of St. Andrews Bay, just inland from the white-sand beaches of Panama City Beach — and the broader area offers a mix of waterfront parks, natural areas, and classic Florida beach town character that's ideally explored by golf cart.

The Panhandle's emerald-green Gulf waters and sugar-white quartz sand make it one of Florida's most visually stunning coastal regions, and a golf cart gives you the freedom to explore it at your own pace — stopping wherever the view demands.

Exploring the Panama City Area by Golf Cart

From a Panama City base, you're within easy range of St. Andrews State Park — one of Florida's best-loved natural areas, featuring two miles of pristine Gulf beach, nature trails, and excellent snorkeling just offshore at Shell Island. The historic St. Andrews neighborhood along Beck Ave offers boutiques, restaurants, and marina views worth a cruise.

The Hathaway Bridge connects Panama City to the beaches of PCB, and the broader area offers fishing piers, waterfront parks along the bay, and the kind of unhurried Panhandle atmosphere that keeps visitors coming back year after year.
