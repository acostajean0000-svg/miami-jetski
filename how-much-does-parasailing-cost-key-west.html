How Much Does Parasailing Cost in Key West? — 2026

🗓 Updated May 2026 ⏱ 5 min read 📍 Key West, FL

Key West parasailing puts you 600–1,200 feet above the turquoise water with views stretching from the Atlantic to the Gulf of Mexico on a clear day. It's one of the most popular activities in Key West, and several operators compete for business at the Garrison Bight Marina and Sunset Pier. Here's exactly what you'll pay in 2026.

Short answer: Key West parasailing runs $75–$120 per person for a single flight, or $65–$95 per person tandem. Key West prices run slightly higher than Miami due to the destination's premium status.

Tips to Get the Best Price

  • Book the sunset flight: Key West's Mallory Square sunset is world-famous — being in the air during the green flash is worth the $10–$20 premium.
  • Tandem saves 25–30%: Two people on the same bar is $10–$30 total more than a single — book with your partner for the best per-person rate.
  • Garrison Bight has more operators: More competition = better prices. You can often walk up and negotiate, especially on slow weekday mornings.
  • Weather cancellations are common: Key West can have sudden afternoon storms. Book with an operator that offers free reschedules — most do.
  • Book the morning slot if budget matters: Sunrise flights (first boat out at 8am) are often discounted and offer glassy, calm water below.

Frequently Asked Questions

How high do you fly when parasailing in Key West?

Most Key West operators fly at 600–1,200 feet depending on wind and line length. At 1,200 feet on a clear day, you can see both the Atlantic and the Gulf of Mexico simultaneously — one of the most dramatic views available from any non-aircraft activity in the US.

Is parasailing in Key West scary?

Most first-timers are surprised by how peaceful it is. The initial lift from the boat deck is the most exhilarating moment; once airborne it's quiet and smooth. The dip option (briefly touching the water during the flight) is the biggest thrill upgrade available.

Can you parasail in Key West year-round?

Yes — Key West's weather allows year-round parasailing. Winter (November–April) has the most reliable calm conditions. Summer (June–September) is doable in the mornings before afternoon thunderstorms build. Most operators offer full refunds or reschedules for weather cancellations.

What should I wear for parasailing in Key West?

Lightweight clothes you don't mind getting slightly wet (if you choose the dip option). No shoes are worn — you're barefoot on the boat. Sunscreen is required; use reef-safe formulas as Key West is a marine sanctuary.
