🛺 About This Tour

Roll through 500 years of American history on the Shared Golf Cart Tour with St. Augustine Land & Sea Tours. Departing from St. George Street — the beating heart of the nation's oldest city — you'll glide through cobblestone lanes and colonial-era streets in an open-air golf cart guided by someone who truly knows this town.


St. Augustine was founded in 1565, predating Jamestown by 42 years, and every block of the historic district holds a story. Your guide weaves through the Castillo de San Marcos — the oldest masonry fort in the continental US — past the haunted halls of Flagler College, the narrow streets of the colonial quarter, and the oldest wooden schoolhouse in America. It's a comfortable, accessible way to take in a massive amount of history without wearing out your feet.


The shared format keeps prices low while still delivering a personalized, engaging experience. Great for solo travelers, couples, families, and anyone visiting St. Augustine for the first time. Book via FareHarbor for live availability and instant confirmation.
