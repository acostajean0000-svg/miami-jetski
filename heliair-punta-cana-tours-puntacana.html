FAQ – HeliAir Punta Cana Tours

Is any prior experience needed for aerial activities? +
No experience needed. All necessary training is provided on-site.
What's the weight limit for helicopter tours? +
Typically 250 lbs (113 kg) per passenger. Confirm at time of booking.
Are aerial photos included? +
Most operators include professional photos or allow personal cameras.
What's the minimum age for flights? +
Children 2+ can join as passengers on helicopter tours. Zip lines typically require 8+.
What if I'm afraid of heights? +
Start with a shorter tour to build confidence. Guides are trained to assist nervous first-timers.
