Good to know

San Diego enjoys mild weather all year; summer is warmest, gray-whale watching peaks December–April and blue whales appear in summer. Bring a hat, sunscreen and snacks; licenses are usually included on the boat. Frogwater departs from San Diego, and pairs well with a visit to Mission Beach.
