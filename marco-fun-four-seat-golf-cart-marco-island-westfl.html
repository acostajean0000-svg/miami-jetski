🛺 About This Rental

Marco Island is a jewel of Southwest Florida — a barrier island with wide, bikeable streets, stunning Gulf beaches, and a compact layout that makes a golf cart the single best way to get around. Marco Fun's brand-new four-seat golf cart inventory means you're starting your adventure in a clean, premium ride with no mileage or wear.


Cruise down to Tigertail Beach, one of the most beautiful spots in Florida with its tidal lagoon and sandbar stretching into the Gulf. Swing by Resident Beach for a quieter swim, explore the picturesque waterway-lined streets of the island's residential areas, or grab lunch along Bald Eagle Drive where Marco's best restaurants and shops are clustered. Caxambas Park at the island's south end offers stunning bay views and boat launch access.


Four seats make it perfect for couples, families, or small groups. Ages 21+ to drive. Book via FareHarbor for live availability and instant confirmation.
