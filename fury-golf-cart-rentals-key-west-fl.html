About Fury Golf Cart Rentals – Key West

Fury Golf Cart Rentals is operated by Fury Water Adventures, one of Key West's most recognized and trusted activity companies. Exploring Key West by Golf Cart

Key West is tailor-made for golf cart exploration. The island is only about 4 miles long and 2 miles wide, making it easy to see everything without fighting car traffic or hunting for expensive parking. Starting from Fury's Front Street location near the Key West Bight Marina, you can roll to the Southernmost Point buoy, cruise Duval Street, visit the Ernest Hemingway Home, and make it to the beaches at Fort Zachary Taylor – all in one afternoon.

The Greene Street location puts you right in the heart of Old Town, just steps from Mallory Square and the waterfront. Both locations are centrally positioned for exploring every corner of Key West.
