Family Jet Ski Tips — What to Know Before You Go

Age rules: Drivers must be 16+ with a valid government-issued photo ID. Passengers can be any age. No exceptions on the age-16 driving rule — it's Florida law. Kids ride tandem with an adult driver.

Best time for families: Weekday mornings (9–11am) are less crowded and the water is calmest. Biscayne Bay stays flat almost any day. Avoid weekend afternoons when boat traffic picks up.

What to bring: Reef-safe sunscreen, rash guards or wetsuits for sun protection, water shoes, a waterproof phone case, and a change of clothes. Life jackets are provided — but bring your own if your child uses a specific fit.

How long to book: 1 hour is plenty for young kids (under 8). 1.5–2 hours works well for older kids and teens who want more time on the water. Most operators sell time in 30-minute increments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum age for jet ski rental in Miami?

Drivers must be 16+ with valid ID. Passengers (riding tandem) have no minimum age — young kids can ride with a parent. Always confirm with your specific operator.

Are jet ski rentals safe for families with young kids?

Yes — especially on calm Biscayne Bay with Miami Style Rentals. Guided group tours, mandatory life jackets, and protected water make it suitable for families with children of all ages.

What should families bring to a jet ski rental?

Sunscreen, water shoes, a change of clothes, and a waterproof bag for phones. Life jackets are provided. Arrive 15 minutes early to get kids fitted with the correct PFD size.

Can a non-driver parent ride with their child on a jet ski?

Yes. A licensed adult can drive a tandem jet ski with a child as passenger. Miami Style Rentals and North Miami Beach are the best options for this.

Which Miami jet ski rental is best for families?

Miami Style Rentals on Biscayne Bay — calm water, guided tour, tandem skis, and the highest rating of any Miami operator at 4.8 stars with 3,200+ reviews.
