Idle Speed Water Sports sits at 663 Bay Esplanade on the north end of Clearwater Beach island — away from the dense resort strip, closer to the water, and perfectly positioned for exploring both the beach side and the bay side of one of Florida's most celebrated beach destinations. Their brand-new Premium Lifted Golf Carts are a step above the standard beach cart: elevated suspension, premium finish, and built to make every cruise look and feel great.
