About exotic car rentals in Miami

Miami is one of the top markets in the United States for exotic and supercar rentals — driven by year-round sunshine, palm-lined boulevards like Collins Avenue and Ocean Drive, and a strong luxury-tourism culture. Whether you're celebrating a milestone, attending a Miami event, or staging a photoshoot, renting a Lamborghini Huracán, Ferrari 488, McLaren 720S or Rolls-Royce Cullinan is a quintessential South Florida experience.

Popular Miami exotic-car routes

  • Ocean Drive — the iconic Art Deco strip in South Beach, perfect for slow cruising.
  • MacArthur Causeway — open Miami Beach skyline run from downtown to South Beach.
  • Star Island & Palm Island — mansion-lined residential islands; popular for car photoshoots.
  • Brickell & Coconut Grove loop — downtown skyline, marinas, waterfront restaurants.
  • Key Biscayne & Crandon Boulevard — bridge-and-causeway drive with skyline views.

What to expect when booking

Most Miami exotic-car operators require renters to be 21+ (often 25+ for top-tier supercars), with a valid driver's license, proof of full-coverage insurance, and a credit card for the security deposit. Rental durations range from 1 hour (entry rate) up to multi-day packages with delivery to Miami Beach hotels. Multi-day discounts and weekend packages are common.
