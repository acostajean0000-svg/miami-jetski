Where to ride a jet ski in Miami

The 19 operators on this page launch from three areas: Miami (13), Miami Beach (4), North Miami (2).

Biscayne Bay and Downtown Miami

Most rentals leave from marinas around Bayside Marketplace and the Venetian Causeway. Rides cross Biscayne Bay with views of the downtown skyline, Star Island and the islands along the causeways. The bay is sheltered, which makes it the usual choice for first-time riders.

Miami Beach, Sunny Isles and Haulover

North of South Beach, operators run from the Haulover Inlet area and the Intracoastal near Sunny Isles. Expect sandbar stops and more open water when the wind picks up.

Key Biscayne

Rentals near the Rickenbacker Causeway head out to the waters around Key Biscayne, with calmer coves and views back toward the city.

Jet ski rules in Florida

  • Age: you must be at least 18 to rent a personal watercraft, and at least 14 to drive one.
  • Boating education: anyone born on or after January 1, 1988 needs a Florida Boating Safety Education ID card, or a temporary certificate, to operate a vessel of 10 hp or more. Rental operators can issue the temporary certificate after a short test before your ride.
  • Life jackets: everyone on a jet ski must wear a U.S. Coast Guard–approved, non-inflatable life jacket, and the driver must attach the engine cut-off lanyard.
  • Daylight only: jet skis may not be operated from half an hour after sunset to half an hour before sunrise.
  • No-wake and manatee zones: parts of Biscayne Bay are slow-speed zones; stay inside the riding area your operator shows you.

Rules summarized from Florida boating law; check the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission for the official text.

Tips before you book

  • Mornings usually have the calmest water on the bay; afternoons get choppier when the sea breeze builds.
  • Check whether the price is for one rider or two, and whether it is a free ride or a guided tour.
  • Most operators take a refundable damage deposit and ask for a photo ID at check-in; arrive 15 minutes early.

Most Miami jet ski rentals start around $99 for 30 minutes. Hourly and tour packages are available — compare live prices on the map and cards above.

Do I need a license to rent a jet ski?

No boating license is required to rent, but Florida law requires anyone born on or after Jan 1, 1988 to complete a free boating safety course — most operators handle this on-site in minutes.

What's the minimum age?

You typically must be 18+ to rent and drive solo (with a valid photo ID). Younger riders can usually ride as passengers with an adult.
