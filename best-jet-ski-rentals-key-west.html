Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the best place to jet ski in the Florida Keys?

Grassy Key and Marathon (MM 47–58) offer the best combination of price, water quality, and open space. The bay side has shallow, clear flats; the Atlantic side has deeper open water. Key West is more expensive but puts you in a more destination setting.

How do the Florida Keys compare to Miami for jet skiing?

The Keys generally offer clearer water and more open space than Miami's Biscayne Bay. Miami has more operators and shorter drives for most visitors. The Keys (especially Marathon area) are worth the extra 1–2 hour drive for the quality of the water.

Can you jet ski near the Seven Mile Bridge?

Yes — Marathon operators offer tours along the Seven Mile Bridge (MM 47). The bridge area has calm water on the Gulf side and deeper Atlantic on the other. The views of the historic old bridge alongside the new bridge are spectacular.

Do Keys jet ski operators require a boating certificate?

Yes — Florida law requires drivers born on/after January 1, 1988 to carry a state-approved boater safety ID card. The free online course takes about 3 hours at BoatUS.com. Operators check this before letting you ride.
