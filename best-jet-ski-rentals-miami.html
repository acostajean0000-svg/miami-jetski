Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best jet ski rental in Miami?

Miami Style Rentals in North Miami is the highest-rated operator in Miami — 4.8 stars with 3,000+ reviews, guided tours through Biscayne Bay, and the best prices starting at $99/hr. For South Beach location, flowvip is the top pick.

Are jet ski rentals in Miami worth it?

Absolutely. Biscayne Bay offers one of the most scenic jet ski environments in the US — the Miami skyline, Haulover Inlet, and open Atlantic access depending on your operator. Guided tours with Miami Style Rentals are especially popular with first-timers.

How far in advance should I book a jet ski in Miami?

1–2 weeks during peak season (December–March). Off-season you can often book same-day. Weekend slots in winter fill fastest — book by Thursday for weekend availability.

Which Miami jet ski rental is best for beginners?

Miami Style Rentals (guided tours in calm Biscayne Bay) and the North Miami Beach intracoastal option are best for first-timers. South Beach operators launch directly into Atlantic chop which can be challenging for beginners.
