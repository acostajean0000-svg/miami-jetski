About Island Style Ride – Key West

Island Style Ride is Key West's versatile rental service offering golf carts, electric cars, scooters, and bikes – all delivered free to wherever you're staying. Whether you roll off the cruise ship, land at Key West International Airport, or check into an Old Town Airbnb, they'll have your cart waiting for you.

Their 4-seater golf carts are perfect for couples, small families, and anyone who wants to explore Key West at a relaxed pace without fighting traffic or hunting for parking on Duval Street.

Fleet Options

  • 4-seater golf carts (street-legal)
  • Electric cars (street-legal low-speed vehicles)
  • Scooters (solo & dual)
  • Bikes (standard & electric)

Free Delivery Service

Island Style Ride delivers anywhere in Key West and Stock Island – your hotel, Airbnb, vacation rental, the Key West cruise port, the ferry terminal, and Key West International Airport. When your rental ends, they pick up the vehicle from the same location. No wasted time, no extra charges.
