Planning Your Miami Bachelorette Water Day

Jet ski vs. party boat: For groups of 4–10, multiple jet skis are the most fun — everyone rides simultaneously and the group energy is high. For groups of 10+, a private party boat gives everyone space to move around, bring drinks, and enjoy the views without splitting up between skis.

Timing: Morning slots (9–11am) have the calmest water and the best photos before the midday glare. Afternoon slots (3–5pm) are popular for the sunset lead-up. Book a jet ski in the morning and save the sunset cruise for the evening.

What to wear: Wear or bring a swimsuit, rash guards for sun, and water shoes. Avoid loose accessories (earrings, bracelets) that can be lost in the water. Waterproof pouches for phones are essential — the group shots from the water are always the best photos of the trip.

Booking tip: Call ahead for large groups to confirm they can accommodate your full group simultaneously. Miami Style Rentals is known for being the most reliable for large bachelorette groups. Book 3–4 weeks out for weekend dates from October–April.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best bachelorette water activities in Miami?

Group jet ski rentals, private party boat charters, parasailing, and sunset yacht cruises. For groups of 4–8, multiple jet skis at Miami Style Rentals is the most popular and affordable. For groups of 8+, a private party boat gives everyone space.

How many people can do a group jet ski rental?

Most operators can handle groups of any size — they have multiple skis available. Miami Style Rentals and Falcon's are the best for large bachelorette groups. Book 3–4 weeks ahead for groups of 6+.

How far in advance should I book?

3–4 weeks for groups of 6+ on weekends, especially October–April. Summer weekdays are easier to get last minute.

Is jet skiing good for a bachelorette party?

One of the top-rated bachelorette activities in Miami. Everyone rides at the same time, the skyline photos are incredible, and the energy is contagious. Most groups do 1.5–2 hours.

Can you rent a party boat in Miami for a bachelorette?

Yes — private party boat charters for 10–30 people are widely available. You can bring your own decorations, music, and drinks. Search FareHarbor for Miami yacht charter options.
