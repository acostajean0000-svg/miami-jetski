About Keys Kartz – Key West

Keys Kartz is a golf cart rental service based in Key West, Florida, offering visitors a convenient and fun way to explore America's southernmost island city. Key West is compact enough that a golf cart covers the whole island comfortably — from the Atlantic beaches and the Southernmost Point on the southern end, to Mallory Square and the historic waterfront on the Gulf side, to the winding lanes of Old Town in between.

With its warm tropical climate, vibrant restaurant and bar scene, and a laid-back attitude that's purely Key West, an afternoon or full day with a rented golf cart is one of the most enjoyable ways to experience the island — rolling from attraction to attraction at your own pace with the salt air and blue sky overhead.

Exploring Key West by Golf Cart

Key West is only about 4 miles long and 2 miles wide, making it ideally sized for golf cart exploration. The main tourism corridor runs along Duval Street from the Gulf to the Atlantic — but the real magic of Key West is in the quieter side streets: Whitehead, Fleming, and Simonton lined with pastel Victorian "conch houses", hidden gardens, and local cafes that most visitors never find.

  • Southernmost Point buoy — one of the USA's most photographed landmarks
  • Ernest Hemingway Home & Museum — 907 Whitehead St
  • Mallory Square — nightly sunset celebration
  • Fort Zachary Taylor State Park — best beach in Key West
  • Old Town Victorian conch house neighborhoods
