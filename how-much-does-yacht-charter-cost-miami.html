How Much Does a Yacht Charter Cost in Miami? — 2026

🗓 Updated May 2026 ⏱ 5 min read 📍 Miami, FL

Miami is one of the world's top luxury yachting destinations — home to the Miami Boat Show, Haulover Inlet (where the big boats run), and a charter industry serving everyone from bachelorette groups to Fortune 500 companies. Prices span an enormous range. This guide covers real 2026 Miami yacht charter rates for all boat sizes and types.

Short answer: A Miami yacht charter starts around $500–$1,000 for a 3-hour trip on a 35–45 ft yacht. Full-day luxury charters on 60–80 ft yachts run $3,000–$8,000+. All prices below include a licensed captain.

Tips to Get the Best Price

  • Price is per boat, not per person — a 12-person yacht at $1,200 costs $100/person when filled. Group charters are often cheaper than people think.
  • What's not in the price: Fuel ($150–$600), dock fees ($50–$200), captain gratuity (15–20%), catering ($30–$100/person), and alcohol are typically extra.
  • Compare all-in price: A 'cheap' $800 charter with $400 in extras can cost more than a $1,200 'all-inclusive' charter. Always ask for a total cost breakdown.
  • Shoulder season (May, October): Miami yacht charter prices drop 15–25% outside the December–April peak season.
  • Bimini day trips are the upgrade: A 45-minute offshore run from Miami to Bimini, Bahamas turns a standard charter into an international trip — ask operators about this option.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in a Miami yacht charter?

Most Miami yacht charters include a licensed captain, fuel within a standard range (Biscayne Bay to the sandbar), safety equipment, and basic towels/ice. Catering, alcohol, dock fees, and extended fuel costs are usually extra. Always confirm what's included before signing.

How many people can a Miami yacht charter hold?

Depends on vessel size — 35 ft yachts typically accommodate 6–12 guests, 60 ft yachts fit 15–20, and mega-yachts over 80 ft can hold 30–50+. USCG certification sets the legal maximum per vessel.

Do I need to bring food and drinks on a yacht charter in Miami?

Most operators allow you to bring your own food and beverages (BYOB). Some offer catering add-ons ($25–$100/person). For a large party, BYO is typically more economical. Check whether the vessel has a refrigerator and wet bar — most do.

What's the best area to yacht in Miami?

Biscayne Bay is ideal for calm-water cruising and sandbar stops (Nixon Sandbar is the social hub). Haulover Inlet leads to the Atlantic for offshore runs. Key Biscayne and Stiltsville are popular stops. Bimini, Bahamas (45 min offshore) is the ultimate day-trip destination for larger vessels.
