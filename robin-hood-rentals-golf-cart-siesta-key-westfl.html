Exploring Siesta Key by Golf Cart

Siesta Key is a barrier island off Sarasota, home to one of the world's top-rated beaches – Siesta Key Beach, known for its powdery white quartz sand. A golf cart lets you skip the parking drama entirely and roll from Siesta Key Village to the beach, the bayside, and back whenever you like.

Popular spots to cruise to include Siesta Key Village (restaurants, bars, shops), Siesta Key Beach, Turtle Beach, and the waterfront parks along the bay side of the island.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Robin Hood Rentals on Siesta Key?
5255 Ocean Blvd, Siesta Key, FL 34242 – right in Siesta Key Village. Phone: (941) 554-4242.
What is the best time to rent a golf cart on Siesta Key?
Mornings are ideal – the village is cooler and parking at the beach is still manageable. Sunset rides along the bay side are also a local favorite.
Can I ride the golf cart on the beach?
Golf carts are street-legal vehicles and cannot be driven on the sand. However, you can park right at beach access points and walk in – a huge convenience.
Does Robin Hood Rentals offer scooters too?
Yes – Robin Hood rents motor scooters, scooter cars, bicycles, segways, paddleboards, and more in addition to golf carts.
