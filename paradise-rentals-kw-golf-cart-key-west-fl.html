About Paradise Rentals KW

Paradise Rentals KW is Key West's go-to source for premium electric golf carts, offering free delivery and pickup to hotels, Airbnbs, vacation rentals, the cruise port, and the Key West Airport. Explore the island at your own pace in a quiet, eco-friendly cart loaded with Bluetooth radio and USB charging ports.

Choose from a 4-person cart (from $149) or a 6-person cart (from $199) for groups. Rental periods range from 4 hours to a full week – perfect for a quick day out or an extended island stay.

Things to Do in Your Golf Cart

A golf cart is the perfect way to see Key West. Drive down Duval Street, loop around the historic district, stop at Mallory Square for sunset, or cruise the quiet streets of Old Town. You can reach beaches, restaurants, and bars without dealing with parking – a huge plus on the island.

Highlights easily explored by cart include: Hemingway Home, Fort Zachary Taylor State Park, the Key West Butterfly Conservatory, Smathers Beach, and the Southernmost Point Buoy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a driver's license to rent a golf cart in Key West?
Yes – a valid driver's license is required. Renters must be at least 22 years old.
Will they deliver the cart to my hotel?
Yes – free delivery and pickup is included to any hotel, Airbnb, vacation rental, cruise port, or the Key West Airport throughout the island.
How much does it cost to rent a golf cart in Key West?
Paradise Rentals KW starts at $149 for a 4-person cart and $199 for a 6-person cart. Multi-day rentals are available at discounted rates.
Are the carts street legal?
Yes – all carts are fully street-legal low-speed vehicles (LSVs) registered and insured for Key West roads.
Can I take the golf cart to other Florida Keys?
Rentals are intended for Key West only. The Overseas Highway is not suitable for low-speed vehicles, so carts are restricted to the island.
