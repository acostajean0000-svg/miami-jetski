About this Experience

Get behind the wheel of a Manual Polaris Slingshot right on Front Beach Road — Panama City Beach's most famous stretch. Bike the Beach PCB puts you directly on the Emerald Coast's iconic strip, with the Gulf of Mexico on one side and the best restaurants, shops, and bars on the other. The manual transmission adds a raw, engaging feel to every shift as you cruise past Pier Park, roll east toward the quieter 30A corridor, or loop the bay-side roads at sunset. From $250, it's the most exciting way to see PCB.

Frequently Asked Questions
Florida classifies the Slingshot as an autocycle. For a manual transmission model, a standard driver's license is required and a motorcycle endorsement may be needed. Confirm with the operator at booking.
The manual Slingshot uses a clutch and gear shifter, giving you full control over acceleration and shifting. It delivers a more engaging, sports-car-like driving experience for those comfortable with manual transmissions.
