About Livin Charters – Nokomis, FL

Livin Charters is a full-service outdoor adventure company located on Casey Key Road in Nokomis, Florida, serving the south Sarasota coast with street-legal golf cart rentals, luxury yacht charters, offshore fishing charters, and more. Their Casey Key Road waterfront location puts you right on the Gulf Coast, surrounded by the natural beauty of this quieter stretch of Florida's west coast.

Their street-legal golf cart rentals are ideal for exploring Casey Key, Nokomis Beach, Osprey, and the Venice area – a scenic stretch of Gulf Coast that's quieter and more laid-back than the busier Sarasota beaches to the north.

Activities at Livin Charters

  • Street-legal golf cart rentals
  • Luxury yacht charters
  • Offshore fishing charters
  • Boat with captain tours

Exploring Nokomis & the South Sarasota Coast

The Nokomis and Casey Key area is one of Florida's most scenic coastal stretches – a narrow barrier island with the Gulf of Mexico on one side and the Intracoastal Waterway on the other. A golf cart is perfect for cruising the length of Casey Key, hitting Nokomis Beach, or rolling south to the historic Venice beachfront for sharks' tooth hunting on the famous Venice Beach.

Whether you're staying on Casey Key, at a Venice rental, or passing through on a longer Florida road trip, Livin Charters offers a relaxed Gulf Coast experience with high-quality rentals and charters in a beautiful setting.
