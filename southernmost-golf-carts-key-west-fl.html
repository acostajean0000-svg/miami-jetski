Explore Key West by Golf Cart

A golf cart is hands-down the best way to see Key West. The island is just 2 by 4 miles, making it perfectly sized for a leisurely cart ride. Cruise through the colorful streets of Old Town, visit the Hemingway Home, roll down to Fort Zachary Taylor State Park, or watch the sunset at Mallory Square – all at your own pace.

With multi-day rentals, you can keep the cart for your entire trip, exploring different neighborhoods, beaches, and dining spots each day without worrying about transportation.
