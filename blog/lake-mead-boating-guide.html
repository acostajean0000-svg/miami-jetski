Lake Mead Boating Guide 2026 — Rentals, Black Canyon & Hoover Dam

America's largest reservoir, 30 minutes from the Strip

Lake Mead pairs Las Vegas convenience with serious water: 290 square miles of desert lake, the towering Hoover Dam, and the emerald water of Black Canyon downstream — one of the Southwest's best kayak day trips.

What to book

  • Boat & pontoon rentals: from marinas near Boulder City and Callville Bay. Half-day from ~$300.
  • Black Canyon kayak tours: launch below Hoover Dam, paddle past hot springs and Emerald Cave. From ~$130 guided.
  • Jet skis: wide-open water with far less traffic than Havasu.
  • Scenic cruises: paddlewheeler trips toward Hoover Dam if you'd rather relax.

Tips

  • Spring and fall are perfect; summer is hot but the water stays warm into October.
  • Emerald Cave glows best late morning — time your kayak trip for it.
  • A National Park entrance fee applies to Lake Mead NRA — bring your pass.
  • Book Black Canyon tours 1-2 weeks out; permits limit daily capacity.
