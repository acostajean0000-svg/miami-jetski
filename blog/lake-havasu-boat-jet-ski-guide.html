Lake Havasu Boat & Jet Ski Rental Guide 2026

The jet ski capital of the Southwest

🛥️ Compare all 5 Lake Havasu jet ski operators on one live map — book online in 2 minutes →

Lake Havasu's 400+ miles of shoreline, 300 days of sunshine and warm desert water make it the place Arizona and California ride. The scene centers on the London Bridge channel and Copper Canyon — spring break's legendary boat-up cove.

What to rent

  • Jet skis / waverunners: newest fleets in the Southwest; open water plus canyon runs. From ~$95/hour, full-day discounts common.
  • Pontoons & deck boats: ideal for families and coves; most include bimini shade. Half-day from ~$350.
  • Performance boats: Havasu is powerboat country — some operators rent them with captain.

When to go & tips

  • March-May and September-October are ideal: hot days, fewer crowds than mid-summer.
  • Summer afternoons hit 110°F — book morning slots.
  • Copper Canyon on summer weekends is a party; for quiet coves head north toward the Sandbar.
  • Arizona requires no boating license for renters — operators give a quick orientation.
