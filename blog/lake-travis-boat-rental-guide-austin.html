Lake Travis Boat Rentals Guide 2026 — Party Barges, Pontoons & Coves

Why Lake Travis is Texas's boating capital

With 63 miles of clear, deep water winding through the Hill Country, Lake Travis is where Austin goes to play. Summer weekends revolve around Devil's Cove — the famous party cove where hundreds of boats tie up — while quieter arms of the lake offer swimming, cliff views and calm coves for families.

This guide covers the main rental types, what they cost, and how to book for 2026.

Pontoon vs party barge vs wake boat

  • Pontoons (8-15 people): the everyday choice — stable, shaded, easy to drive. Typical half-day rates from $400-700.
  • Party barges (15-30+ people): double-decker barges with slides and sound systems, made for Devil's Cove. From $900 half-day; book weekends 2-3 weeks out.
  • Wake & surf boats: for wakeboarding and surfing sessions, usually with captain. Hourly rates from $200.

Most rentals depart from marinas 20-40 minutes from downtown Austin. Lady Bird Lake (downtown) is electric-only — that's where kayaks, SUPs and electric cruises live.

Booking tips

  • Summer Saturdays sell out first — book 2+ weeks ahead for June-August.
  • Texas requires boater education for operators born after Sept 1, 1993 — many companies include a captain, which skips the requirement entirely.
  • Check what's included: fuel, captain, ice and tubes vary by operator.
  • Spring and fall weekdays are 30-40% cheaper with the same weather.
