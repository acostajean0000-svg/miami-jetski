Lake of the Ozarks Boat Rental Guide 2026 — Pontoons, Party Coves & More

The Midwest's summer capital

With 1,150 miles of shoreline — more than California's coast — Lake of the Ozarks is where the Midwest spends its summers. Everything runs on mile markers: restaurants, coves and marinas are all addressed by MM, and your rental operator will give you a map.

What to rent

  • Pontoons & tritoons: the lake standard. Tritoons handle weekend chop much better — worth the upgrade. Full-day from ~$400.
  • Wave runners: best in the quieter arms (Gravois, Niangua) away from big-boat wakes.
  • Party cove trips: Anderson Hollow Cove (MM 34) is the famous anchor-up scene on summer Saturdays.
  • Fishing charters: the lake is a top bass fishery with year-round guides.

Tips

  • Memorial Day-Labor Day is peak; June Saturdays book out first.
  • Weekday rentals are cheaper and the water is far calmer.
  • No boating license needed in Missouri for renters born before 1984; operators handle the safety card process for everyone else.
  • Osage Beach and Lake Ozark have the densest marina options.
