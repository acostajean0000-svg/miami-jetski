Frequently asked questions

FAQs about XCR

Most common questions before booking. If your question isn't here, message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need a driver's license?
Yes. Valid driver's license required (Dominican, US, European or international). Minimum age 18 years. The security deposit is $500 USD refundable when the vehicle is returned in good condition.
Can I book without paying online?
Sí, 0% prepago. WhatsApp booking con tu fecha y vehículo. Te confirmamos disponibilidad y precio. Pagas al recoger el vehículo en efectivo o tarjeta (USD o DOP). Sin penalidad si cancelas hasta 24h antes.
Do you deliver the vehicle to my hotel?
Yes, free to hotels in Bávaro, Punta Cana, Los Corales, Cap Cana and west Punta Cana area. Coordinate delivery via WhatsApp — we'll confirm exact time the day before.
How much does it cost to rent a buggy?
Buggy Polaris 2 seats: $110/2hr. Can-Am 2 seats: $130/2hr. Buggy 4 seats (Can-Am o Polaris): $170/2hr. Tarifas decrecen para alquileres más largos — ver tabla completa de precios arriba.
Are night tours safe?
Yes, all guided tours (day and night) include professional guide, helmet and fuel. The night route passes through lit areas and speed is adjusted. No children under 12 allowed on guided tours.
Can I cancel if something comes up?
Yes, free cancellation up to 24h before the booked date. We don't charge until you receive the vehicle, so canceling is just a WhatsApp message.
Do you accept Dominican pesos?
Yes. We accept USD, DOP, credit and debit cards. If paying in pesos we use the daily exchange rate. No extra fees.
