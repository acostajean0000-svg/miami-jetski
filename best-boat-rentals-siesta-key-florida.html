Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best boat rentals on Siesta Key?

CBS Outfitters has the best pontoon rentals with 4.8 stars across 1,000+ reviews — choose between 20ft (8 people) and 27ft (12 people) boats. For jet ski and waverunner rentals, Siesta Key Watersports is the clear leader with 8,500+ reviews at 4.9 stars.

Can I rent a pontoon boat on Siesta Key without a license?

Yes — Florida allows unlicensed boat rentals for vessels up to a certain size. CBS Outfitters provides a safety orientation before departure. No prior boating experience is required.

Where should I take a pontoon boat on Siesta Key?

Top destinations include the sandbars accessible from Midnight Pass, Turtle Beach, and the Gulf inlets around Siesta Key. Ask CBS Outfitters for a map of the best sandbar spots when you pick up your boat.

Is Siesta Key good for jet skiing?

Yes — Siesta Key is excellent for jet skiing with calm intracoastal water for beginners and Gulf access for experienced riders. The water clarity is exceptional. Book with Siesta Key Watersports for the highest-rated experience.
