How Much Does a Jet Ski Rental Cost in Miami? — 2026

🗓 Updated May 2026 ⏱ 5 min read 📍 Miami

Miami is one of the most popular jet ski destinations in the US — and prices reflect that. Expect to pay more here than anywhere else in Florida, but the experience (Biscayne Bay, the Miami Beach skyline, Haulover Inlet) is worth it. This guide covers real 2026 prices across South Beach, North Miami, Brickell and Hollywood based on our database of 22 jet ski operators in the Miami metro area.

Short answer: Expect to pay $99–$200 per hour in Miami. South Beach is on the high end ($150–$200). North Miami and Brickell average $99–$150. Hollywood (Broward, 20 min south) averages $110–$130.

Tips to Get the Best Price

  • Book weekdays: Weekend prices in Miami are 15–25% higher, especially December–March.
  • North Miami vs South Beach: Miami Style Rentals in North Miami offers comparable ocean access at $50–$70 less per hour than South Beach operators.
  • Group discount: One jet ski fits 2–3 riders — the price is the same regardless of how many people ride.
  • Avoid spring break: March–April prices spike across South Florida. Book at least 2 weeks ahead.
  • Check for combos: Jet ski + boat combo packages offer better total value if you want multiple activities.
  • Skip the damage waiver add-on if your credit card includes rental vehicle insurance — it may cover watercraft.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the jet ski price per person or per ski?

Per jet ski, not per person. A ski that fits 3 riders costs the same as 1 rider. Couples and groups of 3 get significantly better value per person.

What's the cheapest jet ski rental in Miami?

Miami Style Rentals in North Miami starts at $99/hr and offers guided tours of Biscayne Bay. Hollywood Beach operators (Falcon's, Under the Sun) start at $110–$120. South Beach is the most expensive zone.

Do I need a license to rent a jet ski in Miami?

Drivers born on or after January 1, 1988 must complete a Florida-approved boater safety course (free online at BoatUS). Passengers have no license requirement. Minimum driver age is typically 18.

When is the cheapest time to rent a jet ski in Miami?

September–November (post-hurricane season) offers the lowest prices and smallest crowds. Weekdays year-round are 15–25% cheaper than weekends.
