🛺 About This Rental

Marathon sits at the heart of the Florida Keys — MM 50 on the Overseas Highway — with the Atlantic Ocean on one side and Florida Bay on the other. Captain Pip's Golf Carts puts you in the Kokomo 4 Seater, a comfortable, easy-to-drive cart built for exploring every corner of the middle Keys at your own pace.


Cruise down to Sombrero Beach for a swim in the crystal-clear Atlantic, ride out to the iconic Seven Mile Bridge for sweeping ocean-to-ocean views, or hop between the waterfront seafood shacks, dive shops, and tiki bars that make Marathon one of the Keys' most laid-back stops. The cart handles the narrow island roads and parking spots that cars struggle with — and you won't spend a minute looking for a spot.


Flexible rental windows from 4 hours to a full week mean you can plan a quick afternoon outing or use the cart as your daily driver for your entire Keys vacation. Ages 22+. Book via FareHarbor for instant confirmation.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

The Kokomo is a 4-passenger golf cart from Captain Pip's, designed for exploring the Florida Keys. It's comfortable for up to 4 riders and easy to maneuver on the narrow island roads of Marathon and surrounding keys.
Rentals are available from 4 hours to a full week, giving you flexibility for a quick afternoon cruise or a multi-day Keys adventure.
Top spots include Sombrero Beach, the Seven Mile Bridge (both the old and new bridges), Boot Key Harbor, the Turtle Hospital, and the many waterfront restaurants and tiki bars along the Overseas Highway.
Captain Pip's Golf Carts is located at 1480 Overseas Hwy, Marathon, FL 33050 — directly on the main highway through the Keys for easy access.
Renters must be 22 years of age or older with a valid driver's license to rent the Kokomo golf cart.
