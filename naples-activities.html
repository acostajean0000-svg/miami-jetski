Palm Beach — Florida's gold-coast playground

Palm Beach County stretches from Boca Raton in the south up through West Palm Beach, Riviera Beach, Singer Island, and Jupiter. It's the southern Florida pick for travelers who want a calmer, more upscale alternative to the Miami party scene — calmer water, manicured beaches, and the famous Lake Worth Lagoon as your inland playground.

Most water tours launch from Singer Island, Peanut Island, or the Riviera Beach Marina. Jet ski rentals explore the protected lagoon. Yacht and pontoon charters head out the Lake Worth Inlet to Peanut Island for sandbar parties. The area's hidden gem is Phil Foster Park under the Blue Heron Bridge — a shore-snorkel spot with 100+ marine species (seahorses, octopus, batfish) protected by the bridge structure. Several "drift snorkel" charters bookable on this page time their visits to slack high tide.

Beyond the water: the Palm Beaches host one of Florida's most active art and dining scenes (Worth Avenue, downtown West Palm), polo matches in season, and the Norton Museum. Most operators offer pickup from major hotels in West Palm, Singer Island, and Boca. Book at least 48 hours ahead for weekends in season (December-April).
