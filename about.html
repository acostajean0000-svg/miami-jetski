The story behind Florida WaterSports

We're the largest verified marketplace for watersports, tours and rentals across Florida, Hawaii, Cancún and Punta Cana. Every operator on our site is a vetted FareHarbor partner with real-time availability, secure online checkout and verified customer reviews.

We built this site to solve that — one place to compare real, bookable operators, in real time.

What makes us different

Every operator is verified, not aggregated

We don't scrape Instagram or list random businesses. Every operator on this site is a FareHarbor partner — meaning they run a legitimate, insured tour or rental business with a real-time availability calendar, secure online booking, and customer reviews. We only list operators with confirmed availability in the next 7 days.

Transparent pricing, no hidden fees

The price you see on the card is the operator's actual starting price (pulled live from FareHarbor). When you click Book Now, the FareHarbor checkout shows you final pricing — including any fees, taxes, and the operator's cancellation policy — before you commit a dollar.

You book direct with the operator

We don't sit between you and your money. Your payment is processed by FareHarbor (the largest activity-booking platform in the US), going directly to the operator's account. If you have a question, you message the operator directly through FareHarbor's chat. If something goes wrong, FareHarbor's standard refund policy protects you.

Coverage

We currently cover:

  • Florida: Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Palm Beach, Jacksonville, St. Augustine, Daytona, Orlando, Space Coast, Tampa, Naples, Marco Island, Sarasota, Destin, Panama City Beach, Pensacola, the Florida Keys (Key Largo, Marathon, Key West), Crystal River, Silver Springs and the Everglades.
  • Caribbean: Cancún (Mexico), Punta Cana (Dominican Republic).
  • Hawaii: Oahu, Maui, Kauai, Big Island, Molokai and Lanai.

How we make money

We're an affiliate of FareHarbor's partner program — when you book through our site, FareHarbor pays us a small referral fee. This is included in the operator's pricing structure; you don't pay more by booking through us. It's the same price you'd pay on the operator's own site (sometimes less, since some operators offer marketplace-only deals).
