Exploring Clearwater Beach by Golf Cart

Clearwater Beach is consistently ranked among America's top beaches, and there's no better way to experience it than from a golf cart. Cruise Mandalay Avenue, hop between the white-sand stretches of Clearwater Beach and Sand Key to the south, or roll to Pier 60 for the nightly sunset celebration. Adventures Beach Rentals serves all of Clearwater Beach as well as Sand Key – no need to move your rental to get to nearby spots.

With free delivery and pickup included, your cart arrives at your door and gets picked up when you're done. Charge it overnight using the included charger and extension cord that plugs into any standard 110v outlet.
