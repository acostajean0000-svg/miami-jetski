Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Miami JetSki Boat Rentals (“we,” “us,” “our”) operates the website miamijetskiboatrentals.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our site, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are based in the United States; if you visit from outside the U.S., your information is processed in the U.S. where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

Information we collect

  • Usage data — pages viewed, time on site, browser type, approximate location (derived from IP), and similar information collected automatically by Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads conversion tracking.
  • Booking referrals — when you click a “Book Now” button, you're sent to FareHarbor.com to complete the booking. We pass along an affiliate reference (ref=miamistylerentals) so FareHarbor can attribute the booking to us. We do not see your name, email, payment card, or other booking-form data — that is collected by FareHarbor and the operator directly.
  • Newsletter signup (optional) — if you submit your email address through our newsletter form, we use it solely to send you occasional updates and promotions. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.
  • Cookies and local storage — we use small data files stored in your browser to remember your preferences (e.g. comparison list, recently viewed operators) and to support our analytics and advertising partners. See "Cookies" below.

How we use this information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website.
  • To measure traffic, understand how visitors use the site, and improve content (Google Analytics).
  • To deliver more relevant advertising and measure ad effectiveness (Google Ads, Meta Pixel where active).
  • To send newsletters you've opted into.

Who we share it with

  • FareHarbor — when you click through to book, we share only the affiliate reference; no personal details.
  • Google — Analytics and Ads, in aggregate or pseudonymous form.
  • Vercel — our hosting provider, which processes standard request logs.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

You can disable cookies through your browser settings. If you do, some parts of the site (comparisons, recently viewed, the map) may not work correctly. When you first visit, you'll see a cookie banner — declining blocks our analytics and advertising cookies but keeps essential functional ones.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict our use of personal information about you, and to object to processing. To exercise these rights, email privacy@miamijetskiboatrentals.com. We respond within 30 days.

California residents (CCPA) — you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the “sale” of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

EU/UK residents (GDPR/UK GDPR) — our legal basis for processing is your consent (for analytics/advertising cookies and the newsletter) and our legitimate interest in operating and securing the site.

Children

Our site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

The site is served entirely over HTTPS and uses a strict Content Security Policy. We do not store credit-card or other payment data on our servers — payments are handled by FareHarbor and the booking operator.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will always reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@miamijetskiboatrentals.com.
