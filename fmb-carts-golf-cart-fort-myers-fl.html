About FMB Carts – Fort Myers Beach

FMB Carts (Beach Carts of FMB, Inc.) offers premium street-legal golf cart rentals serving Fort Myers Beach, Sanibel, Captiva, and all of Fort Myers with free delivery and pickup. Choose from 4-seater or 6-seater premium gas or electric carts – all fully street-legal and ready to explore the beautiful Lee County barrier islands.

Exploring Fort Myers Beach by Golf Cart

Fort Myers Beach is a 7-mile barrier island with beach access points, seafood restaurants, and the lively Times Square entertainment district at the north end. A golf cart makes it easy to cruise the length of Estero Blvd, hop between beach spots, and avoid parking hassles at popular destinations.

FMB Carts also serves Sanibel and Captiva islands, where golf carts are the preferred mode of transport for shell hunters, birders, and families exploring the J.N. "Ding" Darling Wildlife Refuge and pristine beaches.
