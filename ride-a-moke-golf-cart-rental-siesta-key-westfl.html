🛺 About This Rental

Siesta Key is one of Florida's most beloved barrier islands — home to powdery white quartz sand, crystal-clear Gulf waters, and a relaxed village vibe that's best soaked in at your own pace. Ride A Moke puts you in the driver's seat of a 6-passenger golf cart so you and your crew can explore every corner of the island without the hassle of parking or traffic.


Flexible rental windows from 24 hours to 4 full weeks make this perfect whether you're here for a long weekend or an extended Gulf Coast escape. Cruise from Siesta Key Beach — consistently ranked one of America's finest — down to Crescent Beach, swing through the boutiques and restaurants of Siesta Key Village, or simply wind through the island's quieter residential streets with a sunset view over Sarasota Bay.


With seating for up to 6 passengers and ample storage for beach gear, coolers, and bags, the Ride A Moke cart handles everything a full day on Siesta Key can throw at you. Ages 21+ to rent. Book via FareHarbor for instant confirmation and live availability.
