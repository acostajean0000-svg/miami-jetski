Helicopter aerial tours over Tampa Bay are top-rated. Clearwater Beach jet ski rentals are most popular in summer. ATV and slingshot rentals in St. Pete are great for groups.

Yes — Clearwater Beach has jet ski rentals, parasailing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and boat charters. Multiple operators offer same-day booking.
