How the Miami Bloom Bar works

The build-your-own bouquet bar is the centerpiece of any modern South Florida party. We roll in a flower cart loaded with white buckets full of fresh-cut roses, hydrangeas, daisies, delphinium and lush greenery. Your guests pick their stems, our attendant wraps them in luxe tissue paper or your chosen upgrade (mason jar / glass vase), and they walk away with a custom 9-stem bouquet to take home — usually about 553 total stems for 80 guests.

It's an experience, a party favor, and a built-in photo backdrop all in one. No two bouquets are alike. The fragrance of fresh-cut Miami roses fills the room. Your guests post it on Instagram. Your venue manager asks for our card.

Bouquet generator — what 80 guests looks like

Total transparency on what we bring. For an 80-guest event we source roughly 553 stems — 290 roses (1–2 colors), 64 pom cushion, 64 pom daisy, 75 salal, 40 blue delphinium, 20 pittosporum, and optionally 25 mini carnations. Each guest builds a full 9-stem bouquet (2-3 roses as focal, 3-4 accent flowers, 2-3 greenery stems).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Bloom Bar?
A Bloom Bar (also called a flower bar or bouquet station) is a build-your-own-bouquet experience for events. Guests pick from buckets of fresh-cut roses, hydrangeas, daisies, delphinium and greenery, then wrap a custom bouquet to take home. It's the most-talked-about activation at weddings, baby showers, bridal showers, Mother's Day brunches, corporate events, retail openings and street fairs.
How much does a Bloom Bar cost in Miami?
Our Bloom Bar packages start at $225 for a 20-mini-bouquet station and $350 for a full 30-guest build-your-own setup with attendant. Larger guest counts and add-ons (white cart, mason jars, glass vases, marquee backdrops) are quoted on request. Standalone bouquets and baskets range from $250 to $500.
What does the Bloom Bar setup include?
Every package includes fresh-cut premium stems, wrapping paper, ribbon, signage, a 6-ft display table with white buckets, and one uniformed attendant. Setup takes about 30 minutes before your event starts. Delivery within Miami-Dade County is included; Broward and Palm Beach delivery available.
How many guests can the Bloom Bar accommodate?
Our standard packages serve 20 to 80 guests. We have served 100+ guest events with additional attendants and stem counts on request. For events larger than 30 guests, we add one attendant per 25 guests to keep the bouquet line moving.
What events are best for a Bloom Bar?
Weddings, bridal showers, baby showers, gender reveals, birthday parties, milestone anniversaries, Mother's Day brunches, Galentine's Day parties, corporate events, team-building activations, grand openings, retail pop-ups, and street fairs.
How do I book a Miami Bloom Bar?
Tap any "Book on WhatsApp" button on this page. Tell us your event date, headcount, venue, and color palette. We respond within 24 hours with a custom quote. We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance; 4–6 weeks for weddings and peak holidays (Mother's Day, Valentine's Day).
Do you deliver outside Miami-Dade?
Yes — we serve all of South Florida including Broward County (Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Aventura), Palm Beach County (Boca Raton, Delray Beach, West Palm Beach), Coral Gables, Miami Beach, Brickell, Aventura, and surrounding cities. A small delivery fee applies based on zip code.
Can I customize the flower color palette?
Absolutely. Tell us your event's color theme and we'll source stems to match. Popular palettes: classic red, blush and sage, all-white, sunset (peach, coral, gold), monochrome pink, boho neutrals, neon glow. Custom palettes available for an upcharge based on stem availability.
Is there a Valentine's Day or Mother's Day surcharge?
Yes — the week of Valentine's Day and the week of Mother's Day carry a 20% surcharge due to high stem demand from wholesalers. Book at least 4 weeks in advance for these dates to lock in pricing.
Booking policies: Bloom Bar requires at least 2 weeks advance notice (4–6 weeks for weddings and peak holidays). A 50% deposit secures your date; balance due 48 hours before the event.
