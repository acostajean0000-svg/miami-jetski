About this Experience

Cruise the cobblestone streets and scenic bayfront of America's oldest city in an Automatic Polaris Slingshot with Seaside Adventures. Located in the heart of downtown St. Augustine at 606 N Ponce De Leon Blvd, this open-air three-wheeled roadster is the most exhilarating way to soak in the city's Spanish colonial architecture, Castillo de San Marcos, and stunning Atlantic views. Fully automatic transmission — no motorcycle license required. Starting at just $79.99, it's one of Florida's most affordable Slingshot experiences.
