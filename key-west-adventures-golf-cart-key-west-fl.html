About Key West Adventures

Key West Adventures offers golf cart and Jeep rentals on Key West, giving visitors flexible, fun transportation options for exploring the island. Located at 1618 N Roosevelt Blvd, they're conveniently positioned on the main approach road into Key West from the Florida Keys – easy to access whether you're arriving from the airport, the Upper Keys, or the cruise terminal.

With a variety of vehicle types available, Key West Adventures caters to everyone from couples and small families in golf carts to larger groups who want the open-air, island-cruising experience of a Jeep rental.

Fleet Options

  • Street-legal golf carts (4 & 6-passenger)
  • Jeep rentals (open-top, all-island access)
  • Full daily rental options available

Exploring Key West by Golf Cart

Key West's compact size makes it ideal for golf cart exploration. From the N Roosevelt Blvd location, you can reach Old Town, the Southernmost Point, Duval Street, Fort Zachary Taylor State Park, and the beaches of the Atlantic and Gulf sides in just minutes. A golf cart handles narrow lanes and tight parking spots that cars can't manage, and you'll never pay a parking meter.

For those who want a bit more range – or who simply love the open-air Jeep experience – Key West Adventures also has Jeep rentals available for cruising the full length of the island in style.
