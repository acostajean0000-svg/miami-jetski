🏙️ Things to Do in Oahu, Hawaii

Oahu is Hawaii's most visited island, home to Waikiki Beach, Pearl Harbor, and world-class water sports. Top activities include parasailing off Waikiki, guided kayak tours from Kailua, Pearl Harbor history tours, snorkel cruises at Ko Olina, and dolphin & whale safaris on the Leeward Coast with Wild Side Specialty Tours.

🌊 Things to Do in Maui, Hawaii

Maui offers the Road to Hana, Molokini snorkeling, and humpback whale watching (December–May). Top experiences: surf lessons in Kihei with Maui Wave Riders, the 5-line zipline tour through Haiku jungle, scuba diving at Lanai Cathedrals, and the sunset whale watch with Maui Dreams Dive Co.

🌋 Things to Do on the Big Island, Hawaii

The Big Island hosts Kilauea Volcano, Mauna Kea stargazing, and one of the world's only manta ray night snorkel experiences. Don't miss: the Big Island Grand Circle Tour, fat tire e-bike rides through Volcanoes National Park, Kealakekua Bay snorkel at Captain Cook Monument, and the twilight volcano + stargazing tour.
