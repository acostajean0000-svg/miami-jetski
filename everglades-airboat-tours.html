About airboat rides

An airboat is a flat-bottomed boat driven by a large rear propeller — designed to skim across shallow sawgrass marshes where prop boats can't go. Tours typically run 30 minutes to 2 hours and are noisy (ear protection provided). The best alligator viewing is in cooler months (Nov–Apr); summer trips are hotter but include more wildlife activity. Most tours combine the airboat ride with a wildlife show or gator presentation.
