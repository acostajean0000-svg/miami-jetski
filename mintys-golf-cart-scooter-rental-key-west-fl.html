About Minty's – Key West

Minty's Golf Cart and Scooter Rental is a Key West institution located at 601 Truman Ave in Old Town – just steps from Duval Street, the Ernest Hemingway Home, and the heart of Key West's vibrant neighborhood grid. Minty's offers street-legal golf carts and scooters for daily rental, giving visitors a fun and easy way to explore every corner of the island.

The Truman Ave location puts you right in the thick of Old Town, where most of Key West's main attractions, restaurants, and nightlife are within easy rolling distance.

Fleet Options

  • Street-legal golf carts (4-passenger)
  • Scooters (solo and dual)
  • Daily rental options available

Exploring Key West by Golf Cart from Truman Ave

From the Truman Ave location, you're perfectly positioned to explore Key West's most beloved spots. Roll west to the Southernmost Point buoy and the scenic Atlantic-facing beaches, cruise north to Mallory Square for the legendary sunset celebration, or wind through the quiet residential lanes lined with Victorian conch houses that give Key West its unique character.

With a scooter or golf cart from Minty's, you can cover all 4 miles of the island in a relaxed afternoon – stopping at local cafes, galleries, and hidden beach access points that most tourists riding rental bikes and trams never discover.
