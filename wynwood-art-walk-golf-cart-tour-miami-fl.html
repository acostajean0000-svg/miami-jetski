About the Wynwood Art Walk Golf Cart Tour

The Wynwood Art Walk Golf Cart Tour is one of Miami's most unique and highly rated experiences, whisking you through the world-famous Wynwood Arts District aboard a custom golf cart with an expert local guide. Wynwood is internationally renowned for its open-air street art — walls covered in massive, colorful murals by artists from around the globe, a concentration of independent galleries, and a creative energy unlike anything else in South Florida.

With over 2,120 reviews and a 4.8-star rating, this tour consistently ranks among Miami's best activities. The golf cart format means you cover more ground than on foot while keeping the pace relaxed and the commentary rolling — stopping wherever something catches your eye.

What You'll See

The tour takes you through the heart of Wynwood's gallery district, with stops at the famous Wynwood Walls outdoor museum, massive murals along NW 2nd Ave, hidden alley art, and the ever-changing facades of galleries and creative spaces that define the neighborhood. Your guide provides context on the artists, the history of Wynwood's transformation from a garment district into a global art destination, and the stories behind the murals.

  • Wynwood Walls — the iconic outdoor street art museum
  • NW 2nd Ave gallery corridor
  • Hidden alley murals and street art gems
  • Local insight on Miami's creative culture

Why Wynwood by Golf Cart?

Wynwood's streets are dense with art and activity. A golf cart covers the full district in a way that walking tours can't match — hitting the best murals, back alleys, and off-the-beaten-path spots that most visitors never find. It's the perfect activity for first-timers and return visitors alike, and the open-air format means you're fully immersed in the color and energy of the neighborhood the whole time.
