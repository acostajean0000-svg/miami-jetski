Planning Your Miami Birthday on the Water

Group sizes: 4–8 people on multiple jet skis is the sweet spot — everyone participates simultaneously and the group energy is high. For 10+ people, a private boat charter gives everyone space, lets you bring food and drinks, and creates a more party-like atmosphere on the water.

Jet ski vs. boat for birthdays: Jet ski is more active and thrilling — better for an adventure-oriented birthday group. A boat charter is more relaxed and social — better for mixed-age groups or when you want to drink, eat, and celebrate without the physical demands of riding. Many people combine both: jet ski in the afternoon, sunset boat cruise in the evening.

Booking tips: For groups of 6+, call ahead or book online well in advance (2–4 weeks for weekends). Tell the operator it's a birthday — some operators add small touches or can arrange a group photo op on the water. Miami Style Rentals is most experienced with birthday groups.

What to bring: Waterproof phone case (mandatory for group photos), water shoes, sunscreen, a change of clothes. If doing a private charter, bring a cooler with drinks, a birthday cake, and any decorations — most charter captains are used to this and will help you set up.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best birthday water activities in Miami?

Group jet ski tours and private boat charters are the most popular. For groups of 4–8, multiple jet skis with Miami Style Rentals is the top pick. For 10+ people, a private boat charter is the move.

Can I celebrate a birthday on a boat in Miami?

Absolutely. Private boat charters let you bring decorations, food, and drinks. The captain handles navigation while your group enjoys the Miami skyline. From $600 for a private charter.

How much does a birthday boat party in Miami cost?

Private charters start around $400–$800 for 2 hours. Split among a group it's $40–$80 per person. Group jet ski rentals run $99–$125 per ski per hour.

What's the minimum age for jet skiing?

Drivers must be 16+ with valid ID. Passengers of any age can ride tandem. Under-16 guests can ride with an adult driver.

Can I book jet skis for a large birthday group?

Yes. Miami Style Rentals handles groups of any size simultaneously. Call ahead for groups of 6+ to ensure all skis are available at the same time.
