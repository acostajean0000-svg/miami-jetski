❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What outdoor activities can you do in Central Florida?▼
Central Florida offers airboat tours through the Everglades and springs, kayaking on clear springs, fishing charters on lakes, hot air balloon rides, ATV adventures, and natural spring swimming.
What water activities are near Orlando?▼
Within an hour of Orlando you can find clear spring kayaking, airboat tours, lake fishing, paddleboarding, and scenic boat rides in areas like Kissimmee, Leesburg, and Sanford.
How much do Central Florida activities cost?▼
Airboat tours from $25. Kayaking from $30. Fishing charters from $100. Hot air balloon rides from $175. ATV adventures from $75.
Are there natural spring tours near Orlando?▼
Yes — Silver Springs, DeLand, and Crystal River are within 1-2 hours of Orlando with guided kayak and glass-bottom boat tours through crystal-clear freshwater springs.
Can I book Central Florida activities same day?▼
Yes. Most operators offer same-day availability via FareHarbor with instant confirmation. Some popular tours like airboats fill up fast — book in advance.
