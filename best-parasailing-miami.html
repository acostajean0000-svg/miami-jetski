Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best parasailing in Miami?

Top Line Watersports at South Beach is the highest-rated Miami parasailing operator — great location, 800 ft altitude option, and strong safety record. For the best price, Miami Style Rentals' Bay side parasail starts at $65/person.

Is parasailing in Miami worth it?

Absolutely — the view of South Beach from 800 feet is one of the most iconic perspectives available of Miami. On a clear day you can see from the Port of Miami to Aventura. It's also surprisingly peaceful once you're airborne — less intense than most people expect.

What's the difference between parasailing at South Beach vs Biscayne Bay?

South Beach provides ocean views — you're flying over open Atlantic water with the beach and Art Deco buildings below. Biscayne Bay gives you calmer conditions and a backdrop of the Miami downtown skyline. Both are excellent; South Beach is more iconic, Biscayne Bay is more serene.

How long is a parasailing flight in Miami?

Air time is typically 8–12 minutes per flight. The total experience (check-in, safety briefing, boat ride, flight, boat return) takes about 1–1.5 hours. Most operators run back-to-back flights so you can often walk up and go within 30–45 minutes of arrival.
