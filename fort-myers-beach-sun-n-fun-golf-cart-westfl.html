Exploring Fort Myers Beach by Golf Cart

Fort Myers Beach is a 7-mile-long barrier island (Estero Island) in Lee County – perfectly sized for a golf cart adventure. Drive the full length of Estero Blvd, stop at Times Square, explore the residential north end, grab fresh Gulf seafood, and catch the famous FMB sunset without worrying about parking.

Popular spots by cart: Times Square (shopping & dining hub), Lynn Hall Memorial Park, Lovers Key State Park, Salty Sam's Marina, and the public beach access points along the Gulf.
