Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best boat tour in Fort Lauderdale?

Staying Afloat Party Boat is the most popular shared experience with 1,000+ reviews — great for social groups. For private charters with a focus on the iconic mansion/estate waterway route, Boat Tours Fort Lauderdale is the top pick.

What can you see on a Fort Lauderdale boat tour?

The New River and Intracoastal route shows multi-million dollar estates, mega-yachts docked at private homes, Las Olas Boulevard restaurants, and the Port Everglades shipping channel. Sunset tours add golden-hour lighting to the mega-yacht display. It's often called the 'Venice of America' route.

Are Fort Lauderdale boat tours good for non-swimmers?

Absolutely — most Intracoastal tours are sightseeing-focused with optional swimming stops at calm sandbar areas. Life jackets are always available. You don't need to swim or have any water experience to enjoy the scenery-focused tours.

What's the best Fort Lauderdale boat tour for a birthday party?

Staying Afloat Party Boat for shared energy, or a private charter through Boat Tours Fort Lauderdale for a more exclusive experience. Private charters allow BYO food/drinks, custom music, and can hold up to 15–20 people. Budget $600–$1,200 for a 3-hour private event charter.
