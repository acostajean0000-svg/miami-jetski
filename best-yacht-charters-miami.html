Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best yacht charter in Miami?

100 Miami Luxury Boat Rental leads for premium events and dedicated luxury experience. Miami Style Rentals has the highest overall rating (4.8) and the best price-to-quality ratio. Both include professional captains and cover Biscayne Bay's top spots.

How many people can go on a Miami yacht charter?

35 ft yachts comfortably hold 6–10 guests. 50 ft yachts hold 10–15. 65 ft yachts accommodate 15–20. For larger groups (20–50 people), look for catamaran or motor yacht charters above 70 ft. USCG passenger limits strictly apply.

What should I look for when booking a Miami yacht charter?

Key factors: captain's local knowledge (Biscayne Bay vs. offshore routes), vessel age and maintenance record (request photos), all-in price transparency (fuel, dock fees, tip), cancellation policy, and whether the operator includes swim platform and water toys. Read recent reviews — charter quality can change with new staff.

Is a Miami yacht charter worth it for a small group of 4–6?

Absolutely — on a 35 ft yacht, a 3-hour private charter at $499–$699 works out to $80–$120/person. That's comparable to a shared boat tour but with complete privacy, your own route, and a captain who focuses entirely on your group. It's one of Miami's best value luxury experiences for small groups.
