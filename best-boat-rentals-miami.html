Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best boat rental in Miami?

Miami Style Rentals is the highest-rated overall operator with 3,000+ reviews — best price, best coverage of Biscayne Bay, and captain included. For luxury yachts and events, 100 Miami Luxury Boat Rental is the top pick.

Where's the best place to go by boat in Miami?

Nixon Sandbar (north of Fisher Island) is Miami's most popular anchoring spot for social gatherings. Stiltsville (off Key Biscayne) is a unique historic site. The Miami skyline from Biscayne Bay at sunset is one of the most photographed views in the city. Most Miami operators cover all three.

Do I need to know how to drive a boat in Miami?

Not with most operators — captains are included on the majority of Miami boat charters. If you want a bareboat (captain-yourself) rental, you'll need a valid boating safety certificate and experience with the vessel type. Pontoons under 26 ft are typically the easiest bareboat option.

What's the best boat rental for a bachelorette party in Miami?

100 Miami Luxury Boat Rental and Epic Hotel operators are best for parties. Look for vessels with a swim platform, music system, and sun deck. Budget $1,200–$2,500 for a 4–5 hour private charter accommodating 10–16 guests including captain and all gear.
