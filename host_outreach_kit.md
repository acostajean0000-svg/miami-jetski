# Refstay — First 5 Hosts Outreach Kit

Goal: sign 5 active Miami Airbnb hosts in the next 14 days. Once 5 hosts each refer ~3 bookings/month, you'll have real revenue data, a few host testimonials, and proof for the next 20.

---

## 1. The three DM templates

Pick by channel and how cold the contact is. Always personalize the bracketed bits.

### Template A — Ultra-short (cold Instagram DM, ~280 chars)

> Hey [Name] — I run Refstay, a Miami platform that pays Airbnb hosts 5% commission when guests book jet skis, boats, or tours through a link in your welcome book. Most hosts earn $50–150/month, zero extra work. Want me to send your custom link? — Jean (refstay.com)

Use when: cold Instagram DM, replies on TripAdvisor reviews, comments on local host posts. Keep it under the IG message preview cutoff so they read it without expanding.

## 2. Target host profile — who to contact

### Green flags (target aggressively)

- **Neighborhood**: Miami Beach, South Beach, Brickell, Edgewater, Wynwood, Coral Gables, Coconut Grove, Key Biscayne, Sunny Isles, Surfside, Bal Harbour, Mid-Beach, Bay Harbor Islands, Fisher Island, Star Island
- **Property size**: accommodates 3+ guests (groups = more activity bookings; solo travelers rarely book jet skis)
- **Trust signals**: Superhost badge OR 50+ reviews with 4.85+ rating
- **Listing keywords**: "waterfront", "water view", "near beach", "marina access", "pool", "rooftop", "perfect for couples/families/groups", "vacation rental"
- **Photos**: outdoor scenes, pool/beach shots, family/group photos rather than business-only minimalism
- **Multi-listing operators**: one contact, many guests — disproportionately valuable
- **Active**: "Responds within an hour" badge, recent reviews (last 30 days)

### Red flags (skip these)

- Long-term-only listings (28+ day minimum stays — no transient guests = no activity bookings)
- Business-travel-focused (corporate boilerplate, "ergonomic desk", no leisure amenities)
- New hosts with <25 reviews (still learning the basics, not ready to add a referral system)
- Already promotes a "concierge" or "activity package" in the listing (likely has an exclusive deal already)
- Non-tourist neighborhoods (West Miami, Hialeah, Doral interior, Kendall)
- Listing dormant for 6+ months (probably not active hosts anymore)
- Listings with sketchy photos / no photos / unedited screenshots — host is unprofessional, will be a support burden

## 3. Where to find them — your outreach channels

Ranked by yield-per-hour, fastest to slowest:

1. **Airbnb search**: airbnb.com → Miami → set dates 2–4 weeks out → filter to "Superhost" + "Entire place" → sort by reviews. Open each listing, find the host name, search their name on Instagram. DM via IG (you can't DM through Airbnb until you've booked).
2. **Instagram hashtag search**: `#miamiairbnb`, `#southbeachrental`, `#miamivacationrental`, `#brickelhost`, `#miamishortterm`, `#miamibeachrental`. Filter by recent posts (last 30 days). DM hosts who are actively posting.
3. **Facebook groups**: "Miami Airbnb Hosts", "Miami Short Term Rental Owners", "South Florida Airbnb Hosts", "Miami STR Hosts Network". Read the rules first, then post a value-first introduction (NOT a pitch). Build presence for a week before DMing members.
4. **Local meetups**: Airbnb host meetups (search Eventbrite + Meetup.com for "Miami Airbnb"), Realtor / property manager events. In-person conversion rate is the highest of any channel.
5. **Local co-host networks**: AirSorted, MasterHost, Hostly, GuestReady — they manage multi-listing portfolios in Miami. One conversation with a co-host network operator = potential access to 50+ listings.
6. **TripAdvisor / Google Maps reviews**: search "best Miami vacation rental" → find reviews → identify host names → cross-reference on IG.
7. **Cold email**: only as a follow-up after IG DM goes unanswered for 7+ days, and only if you can find the email publicly listed.

## 5. When they say yes — the onboarding flow

Have these ready before you start outreach so you don't fumble the first conversion:

1. **Reply within 60 minutes**. Speed = trust. If you make them wait a day, they cool off.
2. **Send their custom link**: `refstay.com/r/[their-first-name]` (or first-name + last-initial if name is common). Generate it via your dashboard's signup form.
3. **Send a 1-page "How to use your link" PDF**: shows them where to put the link (welcome book template, WhatsApp message template, QR code download). I haven't built this yet — flag if you want me to.
4. **Confirm payout info**: PayPal email or Zelle phone. Tell them payouts are monthly on the 1st for the previous month's bookings.
5. **Add to your tracking sheet**, set a 7-day follow-up to check if they've placed the link yet.
6. **At 14 days**: check Plausible for `Referred Visit` events with their slug. If zero, ask them gently if they've placed the link. If they have but no traffic, the issue is guest engagement — offer to send them a QR-code sticker PNG.

## 7. Realistic numbers

Expect ~5–15% reply rate on cold Airbnb-Superhost DMs (this is a high-trust niche, but still cold outreach). Of repliers, expect ~30–40% to sign up. So:

- 20 DMs sent → 2–3 replies → 1 signup
- 50 DMs sent → 5–7 replies → 2–3 signups
- 100 DMs sent → 10–15 replies → 4–6 signups ← this is realistically what you need to do to hit your 5-host goal

If your reply rate is below 5% after 30 DMs, change the template before sending more. If above 20%, you've struck a nerve — pour more time in.
