About yacht charters

A yacht charter is typically a half-day or full-day rental of a captained vessel — usually 40–100ft motor yachts, sailing catamarans, or sport-yacht hybrids. Most charters include a captain and crew; some allow a no-license bareboat option. Pricing varies dramatically by length, season, and itinerary, but transparent quotes are available at the booking step.
