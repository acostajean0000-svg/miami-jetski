Florida yacht charters — captain-included luxury on the water

A Florida yacht charter is the answer for celebrations, photo shoots, or a no-compromise day at the sandbar. Charter sizes range from 30-40 ft day boats (half-day from ~$1,200) to 50-65 ft performance yachts ($3,500-$8,500 full day) up to 80-100 ft superyachts for premium events. Every charter on this page includes captain and crew, fuel for the standard cruise area, ice, water, basic snorkel gear, and one or two coolers.

Primary launch hubs: Miami (Brickell, Coconut Grove, Sunny Isles, Dinner Key), Fort Lauderdale (Las Olas, Bahia Mar, Sea Watch), Palm Beach (Riviera Beach Marina), Naples (Tin City), Marco Island, Key West (Stock Island), and Tampa Bay. Popular destinations include Stiltsville, Haulover Sandbar, Nixon Beach, Peanut Island, Cayo Costa, and the Dry Tortugas overnighters.

Optional add-ons available with most yachts: catering and premium bar service, jet ski tow, paddleboards, water toys, tow tube, professional photographer, custom flower décor (for proposals), and DJ. Tipping the crew 15-20% on the total charter is industry standard. Book 3-4 weeks ahead in season; same-day availability sometimes possible mid-week.

About yacht charters

A yacht charter is typically a half-day or full-day rental of a captained vessel — usually 40–100ft motor yachts, sailing catamarans, or sport-yacht hybrids. Most charters include a captain and crew; some allow a no-license bareboat option. Pricing varies dramatically by length, season, and itinerary, but transparent quotes are available at the booking step.
