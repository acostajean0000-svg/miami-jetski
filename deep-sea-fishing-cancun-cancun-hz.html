FAQ – Deep Sea Fishing Cancún

What fish can I catch in Cancún? +
Sailfish, marlin, mahi-mahi, wahoo, grouper, and snapper year-round.
Is gear included? +
Yes — rods, reels, bait, and tackle all provided.
Do I need a license? +
No personal license needed; the charter operates under its own permit.
Can I keep the fish? +
Most operators allow you to keep the catch or have it prepared for you.
Best time to fish in Cancún? +
Sailfish peak March–June; mahi-mahi and wahoo excellent year-round.
