⚡ About This Rental

Good Vibes Island Rides takes the friction out of golf cart rentals on Siesta Key. Instead of driving to a pickup location, they deliver a fully charged 6-seat electric golf cart directly to you on the island — no logistics, no extra driving, just pure island freedom from the moment your rental starts.


The electric cart is quiet, clean, and perfectly suited to Siesta Key's relaxed atmosphere. Cruise to Siesta Key Beach — consistently ranked among America's finest for its powdery white quartz sand — then wind through the village for lunch, stop at Point of Rocks for some of the best snorkeling in Sarasota County, or head to the north end of the island for sweeping views across Sarasota Bay.


Six seats mean the whole group comes along. Daily rental options make it easy to book for just the time you need. When your day is done, Good Vibes picks it up — simple as that. Siesta Key Island only. Book via FareHarbor for live availability.
