🗓 Updated May 2026 ⏱ 5 min read 📍 Miami, FL

Parasailing over Miami's turquoise waters with the South Beach skyline below is one of the most iconic experiences in Florida. Prices are fairly consistent across operators — this guide breaks down exactly what you'll pay in 2026 and what to look for before booking.

Short answer: Miami parasailing runs $65–$100 per person for a solo flight. Tandem (2-person) flights often run the same total price — making it better value to go with a partner.

Tips to Get the Best Price

  • Go tandem to save: Two people on the same bar usually costs only 20–30% more than a single, splitting the cost significantly.
  • GoPro rental adds ~$30–$50 but gives you the best possible keepsake of the trip — worth it for first-timers.
  • Morning flights are calmer: Wind picks up in Miami afternoons (especially July–September). Morning slots are typically smoother and cheaper.
  • Book combo packages: Several operators bundle parasailing with jet ski or boat tours for 15–25% less than booking separately.
  • Weight limits apply: Most rigs have a combined weight limit of 450–550 lbs per bar.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is parasailing in Miami safe?

Yes — licensed Miami parasail operators are regulated by the Coast Guard and Florida Fish & Wildlife Commission. All boats must be inspected, and operators carry liability insurance. The safety record for properly operated parasailing is excellent.

Do I get wet parasailing in Miami?

Only if you choose the 'dip' option where the operator lowers you to touch the water mid-flight (usually an add-on of $10–$20). Standard flights keep you fully dry.

What's the weight limit for parasailing in Miami?

Most operators allow individual riders up to 250–300 lbs and combined tandem weights of 450–550 lbs. Always confirm with the operator before booking if this is a concern.

Can kids go parasailing in Miami?

Children as young as 6 can parasail if they meet the minimum weight requirement (usually 30–40 lbs) and are accompanied by an adult. Most operators require parental consent for riders under 18.
