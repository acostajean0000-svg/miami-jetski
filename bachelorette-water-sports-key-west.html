Planning Your Key West Bachelorette Water Day

The classic Key West bachelorette water day: Morning snorkeling tour → afternoon jet ski or kayak → sunset catamaran with open bar. This 3-part format is the most popular bachelorette itinerary in Key West and can all be booked through FareHarbor operators in this guide.

Sunset is the crown jewel: The Key West sunset celebration at Mallory Square is a daily event — performers, street food, the whole city. A sunset sailing cruise puts you on the water facing the sunset while the crowd gathers on shore. It's the most photographed bachelorette backdrop in the Florida Keys.

Shared vs. private: Fury and Rhum Runner shared sails start at $45–$55 per person and are great for budget-conscious groups. Private catamaran charters start at $500–$800 for 2 hours and give you complete privacy and BYOB flexibility. For groups of 10+, a private charter often works out to similar cost per person.

Key West vs. Miami for bachelorette: Key West has clearer water, more relaxed "end of the road" energy, and the iconic sunset experience. Miami has bigger nightlife and South Beach glamour. Many groups combine both in a weekend trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best bachelorette water activities in Key West?

Sunset catamaran cruises (most popular), group jet ski tours, snorkeling parties, and private boat charters. The Fury Water Adventures sunset sail is the iconic Key West bachelorette experience.

How far in advance should I book?

4–6 weeks for weekend sunset sailing slots. Key West is one of the top 5 bachelorette destinations in the US — popular slots fill fast, especially October through May.

What is the most popular bachelorette activity in Key West?

The sunset catamaran cruise with Fury Water Adventures — open bar, live music, snorkeling, and the most beautiful sunset backdrop in Florida.

Can you do a private boat charter for a bachelorette in Key West?

Yes. Private catamaran and pontoon charters for 10–30 people start around $500–$800 for 2 hours. You bring the decorations, the captain brings the boat.

Is Key West better than Miami for a bachelorette?

Different vibes. Key West = clearer water, sunset experience, relaxed island atmosphere. Miami = bigger nightlife, South Beach glamour. Many groups do both in a weekend.
