About Monkey Island Adventures – Homosassa

Monkey Island Adventures offers electric golf cart rentals at the beautiful Monkey Island Riverside Resort along the Homosassa River in Citrus County, Florida. Located on Florida's Nature Coast, this is the perfect base for exploring crystal-clear springs, manatee habitats, and the lush waterways of the Gulf Coast.

Their 4-seater electric golf carts are ideal for families and small groups wanting to explore the resort grounds, cruise along the river road, and access nearby Homosassa Springs Wildlife State Park and Crystal River.

Exploring Homosassa by Golf Cart

Homosassa is one of Florida's best-kept secrets – a quiet Nature Coast town known for manatee encounters, world-class fishing, and some of the clearest freshwater springs in the state. A golf cart is a fun, easy way to explore the area around the resort and the riverfront community without the hassle of driving and parking.

From Monkey Island Riverside Resort, you can roll to Homosassa Springs Wildlife State Park to see resident Florida manatees, or head toward Crystal River for snorkeling with wild manatees in Kings Bay. The area is quiet, scenic, and perfect for a relaxed Florida getaway.
